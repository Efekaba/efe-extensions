Bulk Downloader

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Bulk Downloader does not collect, store, transmit or sell any personal data.

  • When you click the extension icon, it reads the current page to list downloadable files.
  • That list is kept only in your browser's temporary memory and is deleted when you close the browser.
  • Your settings (folder name and filters) are stored locally in your browser.
  • The extension makes no network requests except showing previews of the images it found on the page and downloading the files you choose.

There are no servers, analytics, ads or trackers, and nothing is shared with anyone.

Contact: support@trigagames.com

This policy covers the Chrome extension “Bulk Downloader – Images, Links & Files”. This page makes no third-party requests.
