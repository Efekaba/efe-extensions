Site History Eraser

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Site History Eraser uses Chrome's history permission for one purpose: deleting visits to websites you have chosen.

To find those visits, it checks visit addresses on your device, only to match them against your list.

It does not collect, store, transmit or sell your browsing history or any personal data.

Your list of chosen sites is stored in Chrome's storage and syncs only through your own Chrome account if Chrome sync is on.

A simple local counter of removed entries is stored on your device.

The extension makes no network requests.

Contact: support@trigagames.com

This policy applies to the Chrome extension “Site History Eraser – Auto-Delete History for Chosen Sites”, version 1.0.0 and later.
